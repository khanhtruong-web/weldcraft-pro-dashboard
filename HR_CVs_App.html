How to get your Drive Access Token:
  1. Go to Google OAuth Playground.
  2. Under Step 1, scroll down to Drive API v3, select https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, and click Authorize APIs.
  3. Sign in to your Google Workspace account and allow access.
  4. Under Step 2, click Exchange authorization code for tokens.
  5. Copy the Access token (not the refresh token) and paste it below. (Note: Tokens expire after 1 hour)
