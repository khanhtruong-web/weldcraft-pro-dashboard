AWS A1.1 Guideline

Conversion Factor: MPa to psi = 1.450 × 10² (or psi = MPa × 145.038)

AWS Standard Metric Practice

• Under AWS guidelines, metric conversions inside codes are generally "rational" rather than strict math conversions (e.g. 5/32 in. is converted directly to 4.0 mm, rather than 3.968 mm).
